CHA SUNGJAE · LECTURE PPT DESIGN SYSTEM

PURPOSE
- 16:9 technical lectures, tutorials, PBL workshops, and instructor-led follow-along sessions.
- Designed to feel like a restrained Korean fashion-commerce editorial system, not an AI-generated UI kit.

PALETTE
- Default: white canvas, #191919 text, black key rules.
- Neutral hierarchy: #555555, #888888, #C7C7C7, #E0E0E0, #F0F0F0, #F5F5F5.
- Sub-colors only: navy #0B1F3A and information blue #2563EB.
- Screenshots retain their original colors. Do not add colored overlays.
- Do not introduce red, orange, yellow, green, purple, cyan, gradients, or glow effects for decoration.

TYPOGRAPHY
- Authoring preference: Pretendard / Helvetica Neue.
- Current Mac runtime: AppleGothic. PDF export should not mix NanumGothic and NanumBrush family substitutions.
- Slide title 32pt by default; section and cover titles are larger. Body is 19pt by default and never below 16pt; caption is 11-12pt.
- Use weight 600-800. Avoid light body copy.
- Titles are audience-facing conclusions, not production labels.

GEOMETRY
- Sharp corners: 0px by default, 4px maximum.
- Flat surfaces, 1px gray borders, 2px black or blue rules.
- No floating-card dashboard composition. Use editorial columns and horizontal rules.
- Page margins 64px. Spacing follows a 4px base unit.

CORE LAYOUTS
01 Cover: black field, white title, one blue rule, instructor and date at bottom.
02 Section: black or navy field, hour number and one outcome sentence.
03 Definition: large term, plain-language meaning, why it matters, concrete example.
04 Claim: takeaway title, one large claim, one short explanation.
05 Key points: title plus four compact bullets separated by rules.
06 Process: numbered horizontal sequence; no rounded cards.
07 Tutorial map: numbered steps and the exact success signal for each step.
08 Screenshot: 38% explanation / 62% un-tinted actual screen, with step number.
09 Code: 34% explanation / 66% black code field, blue left rule.
10 Exercise: numbered steps, timebox, completion evidence, recovery instruction.
11 Failure/Recovery: black failure column versus blue recovery column.
12 Checkpoint: three questions and explicit evidence required.

CONTENT RULES
- One slide = one teaching job.
- Every new acronym receives a definition slide before first practical use.
- Every tutorial sequence includes: entry screen, action, expected screen, success signal, and recovery route.
- Every lab includes a completion artifact. Every external write includes a Human Approval gate.
- Every external claim or screenshot URL is recorded in the slide speaker notes [Sources] block.

REUSE
- design-tokens.json is the single source of color, typography, spacing, and geometry values.
- design-system.mjs exposes the runtime constants used by PowerPoint builders.
- IPA_LECTURE_TEMPLATE.pptx is a visual layout catalog.
- components/ contains reusable monochrome icons, brand marks, and attributed reference images.
- New deck builders should import design-system.mjs and avoid local color definitions.

REFERENCE NOTE
- This is a Musinsa-inspired educational presentation system based on the user-provided Oppadu reference. It is not an official Musinsa brand guideline or an official Musinsa template.
