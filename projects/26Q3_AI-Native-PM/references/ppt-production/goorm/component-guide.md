# AI Campus Prism Lecture Component Guide

## Visual Direction

- Apple-inspired, photography-first, low-chrome deck.
- Use large Korean headlines, restrained body copy, and clear section rhythm.
- Alternate `canvas` / `parchment` / `darkTile` backgrounds instead of decorative dividers.
- Use Deep Navy `#0A2342` as the persistent accent, with mist navy `#C7D7EA` on dark surfaces.
- Avoid decorative gradients, heavy shadows, and nested card layouts.

## Core Components

### Hero Tile
- Full-bleed light or dark background.
- One short headline, one lead sentence, one metadata line.
- Speaker/date expression must remain visible:
  `20260701(수) 19:00-20:00 차성재 (패션 플랫폼 M사 Agentic AI PM, 시립대/아주대 AI 부문 겸임교수)`

### Section Divider
- Large title centered or left-weighted.
- Minimal subtitle.
- Surface alternates between `darkTile` and `parchment`.

### Role Lens
- Four-column structure: PM, UX/UI Design, Engineering, AI-side/Platform.
- Each column uses a short job-to-be-done, key artifacts, and AI Native keywords.

### Technology Reference
- Screenshot-first layout with source URL and one practical interpretation.
- Keep screenshots cropped and secondary; the message is the operational use case.

### Curriculum Bridge
- Map course keywords to real work:
  RAG, Tool Use, Function Calling, Vector DB, Data Contract, Observability, SLO, GitOps, Guardrail, Red Teaming, DataOps.

### Q&A Slide
- Reserve the last 10-15 minutes.
- Pre-answer the submitted questions, then open live Q&A.

## Slide Rhythm

1. Opening and promise.
2. Why AI changes problem solving.
3. What Agentic AI is.
4. How roles change in the workplace.
5. How AI Campus Prism curriculum maps to real work.
6. Portfolio/project examples.
7. Submitted questions.
8. Live Q&A.
